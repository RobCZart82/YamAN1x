YamAN1x is a project to bring the Yamaha AN1x synthesizer to music software.
The goal is a virtual instrument you can load in REAPER and other compatible
hosts, play from a MIDI keyboard, and use in your projects.

[Magyarul](README_HUN.md)

![Original Yamaha AN1x synthesizer](docs/images/an1x-hardware.png)

*The original Yamaha AN1x hardware.*

## Can I try it yet?

YamAN1x is still in development. There’s no playable plug-in to install.
The first milestone for musicians is to play a note in REAPER and hear sound
generated by the emulator. VST3 and AU are planned formats.
